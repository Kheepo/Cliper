## Getting Started

### What is Virality Clipper?

Virality Clipper is an AI-powered tool that analyzes your videos to identify the most engaging segments and creates optimized clips for social media platforms. It uses advanced machine learning to detect viral potential and provides recommendations for maximum engagement.

### System Requirements

**Supported Video Formats:**
- MP4 (recommended)
- MOV
- AVI
- QuickTime

**File Size Limits:**
- Maximum file size: 500MB
- Recommended duration: 1-30 minutes
- Minimum resolution: 720p

**Browser Requirements:**
- Chrome 90+ (recommended)
- Firefox 88+
- Safari 14+
- Edge 90+

### Quick Start

1. **Upload Your Video**
   - Click "Upload Video" or drag and drop your file
   - Select your target niche (optional but recommended)
   - Wait for upload to complete

2. **Monitor Processing**
   - Watch real-time progress updates
   - Processing typically takes 2-5 minutes
   - You'll see steps like "Analyzing content" and "Generating clips"

3. **Review Results**
   - Download your optimized clip
   - Review virality scores and recommendations
   - Copy suggested hashtags and posting times

## Understanding Results

### Virality Scores

Scores range from 0-10, where:
- **8-10**: Extremely high viral potential
- **6-8**: High viral potential
- **4-6**: Moderate viral potential
- **2-4**: Low viral potential
- **0-2**: Very low viral potential

**Score Factors:**
- Content engagement patterns
- Emotional response triggers
- Trending topic alignment
- Platform algorithm preferences

### Hashtag Recommendations

**Types of Hashtags:**
- **Trending**: Currently popular hashtags
- **Niche-specific**: Relevant to your content category
- **Engagement**: Proven to drive interactions
- **Discovery**: Help new audiences find your content

**Usage Tips:**
- Use 5-10 hashtags per post
- Mix trending and niche hashtags
- Avoid overly competitive hashtags
- Include branded hashtags when relevant

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Upload Problems

**"File too large" Error:**
- Compress video to under 500MB
- Use video compression tools
- Reduce resolution if necessary
- Trim unnecessary content

**"Unsupported format" Error:**
- Convert to MP4, MOV, or AVI
- Use video conversion software
- Check file extension
- Ensure file isn't corrupted

**Upload Stuck/Slow:**
- Check internet connection
- Try uploading during off-peak hours
- Clear browser cache
- Disable browser extensions
- Try a different browser

#### Processing Issues

**"Processing failed" Error:**
- Check if video contains copyrighted content
- Ensure audio is clear and audible
- Verify video isn't corrupted
- Try re-uploading the file
- Contact support if issue persists

**Long Processing Times:**
- Normal processing: 2-5 minutes
- Longer videos take more time
- High server load may cause delays
- Complex content requires more analysis
- Check system status page

**Poor Quality Results:**
- Upload higher quality source video
- Ensure clear audio
- Choose appropriate niche
- Provide more context in description
- Try different segments of your video

#### Playback Problems

**Video Won't Play:**
- Update your browser
- Enable JavaScript
- Check internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try incognito/private mode

**Audio Issues:**
- Check device volume
- Test with other videos
- Update browser
- Check audio drivers
- Try different device

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: How accurate are the virality predictions?**
A: Our AI models achieve 75-85% accuracy in predicting engagement patterns. Results vary by content type and platform.

**Q: Can I process multiple videos at once?**
A: Currently, you can upload one video at a time, but you can queue multiple jobs.

**Q: Do you store my videos?**
A: Videos are temporarily stored during processing and automatically deleted after 30 days.

**Q: Can I edit the generated clips?**
A: The platform provides optimized clips, but you can download and edit them with your preferred video editor.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What languages are supported for transcription?**
A: Currently supports English, Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese.

**Q: Can I process live streams?**
A: No, only pre-recorded video files are supported.

**Q: Is there an API available?**
A: Yes, we provide a REST API for developers. See our API documentation.

**Q: Can I integrate this with my existing workflow?**
A: Yes, through our API and webhook integrations.

### Pricing Questions

**Q: Is there a free tier?**
A: Yes, free users get 5 video processing credits per month.

**Q: What are the paid plan benefits?**
A: Paid plans include unlimited processing, priority queue, advanced analytics, and API access.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes, you can cancel anytime. Your plan remains active until the end of the billing period.

### Content Questions

**Q: Can I use copyrighted music?**
A: We recommend using royalty-free music to avoid platform restrictions.

**Q: What types of content work best?**
A: Educational, entertaining, and emotionally engaging content typically performs best.

**Q: Can I process content in other languages?**
A: Yes, but transcription and analysis work best with supported languages.

**Q: Are there content restrictions?**
A: We don't process content that violates platform guidelines or contains inappropriate material.

### Results Questions

**Q: Why did my video get a low virality score?**
A: Low scores may indicate limited engagement triggers, poor audio quality, or content that doesn't align with current trends.

**Q: Can I request a different clip segment?**
A: Currently, the AI selects the optimal segment automatically. Manual selection is planned for future updates.

**Q: How often are hashtag recommendations updated?**
A: Hashtag databases are updated daily to reflect current trends.

**Q: Can I save my results for later?**
A: Yes, all results are saved in your account history for 90 days.
